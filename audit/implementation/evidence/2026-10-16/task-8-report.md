## Statistical calculation and assumptions

The initial scientific pilot contract requires at least eight independent teachers and two repetitions; subsequent completed teacher totals double. Calibration requires whole-teacher bootstrap SD upper/lower ratio ≤2, last-two-batch per-teacher runtime relative change ≤20%, and an explicit endpoint precision tolerance. It never stops on favored sign or significance. Zero/undefined variance permits equivalent-model assessment or estimation, without inventing an equivalence margin. The engineering count exception never grants eligibility.

The bootstrap uses fixed NumPy seed and whole-teacher resampling. Row variance is calculated after averaging each E row over repetitions, retaining shared-E correlation. The between-teacher variance-component lower bound bootstraps `max(0, variance(teacher means) - mean(within variance)/R)`; row-variance upper bound is the upper percentile of resampled teacher row variances. Both use 95% percentile bounds. The endpoint diagnostic is the largest absolute Monte Carlo standard error among SD/H1/static CI endpoints over ten disjoint bootstrap chunks. The tolerance is a declared measurement-precision target, not an application utility threshold. Student-t sensitivity, degrees of freedom, median, teacher effects, effective count, within and row variances are retained. These conditional finite-sample diagnostics do not establish population coverage or capture unseen tails.

Resolved favorable **or unfavorable** H1 uses `0.5*abs(mean_H1)`. If H1 is unresolved, resolved static-partial versus upper ordinary uses the analogous absolute mean. Otherwise the design is fresh estimation with the completed pilot teacher/repetition counts. With estimable variance, sole H1 uses `ceil((z_0.975 + z_0.8)^2 * sd_upper^2 / delta_plan^2)` and actual `K=max(K_calc, pilot_teachers)`. Additional families require an explicit adjusted level. `n_T=max(1, ceil(100*row_variance_upper/between_variance_lower))`; unresolved denominator uses an explicitly supplied finite estimation row count. No epsilon replacement or division by zero is used.

Resampling power centers the observed teacher distribution, scales it to the conservative SD bound, shifts by the planning effect and simulates fresh `K`-teacher samples with two-sided Student-t rejection. Wilson Monte Carlo intervals are retained. This is a **conditional t-based sensitivity**, not claimed bootstrap-primary power or a proof of 95% coverage. A declared supported inference policy and sufficient resampling/Monte Carlo precision are required; otherwise the decision becomes estimation. The current conservative rule requires the power interval lower bound ≥0.8 to keep the powered label. This affects inference authorization only; no optional stopping or sample-count adjustment based on observed favored sign occurs.

Forecasts retain both approximate and floored counts and the additional time/storage cost. They include complete per-teacher setup, repetitions, evaluation rows, controls/probes/failures via measured enclosing costs, and explicit retries. Memory/time/storage constraints are physical inputs. Infeasible floored workloads block without truncation. Curves at K/2K/4K expose Student-t half-width, normal-approximation detectable effect and cost. Within/between variance and marginal fitting cost produce an integer variance-per-cost repetition recommendation; selected repetitions remain the completed pilot count and any change requires new calibration.
