Continued keep remains a separate G candidate; it never truncates the fixed allocation count. For a practical joint-vs-keep decision, the caller uses the existing B1 adapter from the same original parent with the normal full allowance; the short keep probe is the reference for ranking single-unit probes, not a substitute for a full-allowance B1 comparison.

Known method/stage, parent identities/checkpoint, score hash, selection role, original-manifest row/group counts and fixed counts precede possible training failure. Process RNG restores unconditionally; secondary publication errors do not replace original setup/training errors, and independent cost publication remains attempted.

Child events/checkpoints carry G outcomes and growth metadata. Enclosing call time includes validation, snapshotting, per-child setup, keep/losers/failures, training/G/checkpoints, ranking and child publication. It excludes prior parent training/shared scoring and publication of its own final report/timer. Nested durations are not additive.
