Every M2c slot is immutable before fitting. F-only table/hash plus fixed seeded
sampling binds actual IDs. Insufficient eligibility skips slots without new
replacements. M2b/d retain nominal ineligible unit/subset records. All eligible
M2d pairs are trained; G selects its oracle without an extra fit, and V/E measures
that G-selected procedure. Charge the entire candidate population to its oracle.

The parent/snapshot/scoring prerequisites form a DAG. One legacy primary parent
per teacher/repeat supplies actual snapshots and the late G-selected state. All
children retain checked parent/source/data/table identities. Missing original
actual snapshots stay unavailable. Only failure to reach a requested update
admits the separately named fixed-parent-budget fallback; corruption/interruption
requires recovery, never fallback substitution. Reuse available original states.
The fallback runs one no-early-stop parent with full declared8000 ceiling and
captures exact250/1000. Initial contingency ceiling:8 additional parent procedures
and160 missing-snapshot cells; execute only the missing slots. Every arm within
a fallback coordinate shares its complete parent/treatment. Fallback work remains
visible in standalone and physical costs.

The single variance/discordance extension is pre-expanded as conditional work:
the next four independent primary teachers add240 M2a cells. M2e's five ordinary
controls and head refit also repeat on those teachers (40 procedures,8 heads,
48 solves) so the opportunity comparison retains competent same-teacher controls.
M2b/c/d remain at their initial declared diagnostic counts. If fallback is needed
there too, the combined fallback ceiling is16 parents+320 cells. The extension
does not replace initial failures, depends on the existing Gate A rule regardless
of effect sign, and occurs at most once. Report these conditional maxima separately
from the initial table rather than claiming every entry was fitted.

standalone procedure and physically only once.
