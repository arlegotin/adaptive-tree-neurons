# Costs of a worker denied permission to fit

A cooperative process can start and then be denied permission before its body
or nested fits are authorized. Resource-bound requests can retain these roots
in `previous_unstarted_episodes`, with immutable start/receipt hashes. They
contribute known startup/preflight/reap time once and consume no model-attempt
slot. They are not fabricated into successful or closed search bodies.

The reader requires the exact primary module command, request hash, core-only
environment, source/job/resource binding and operational-history prefixes. It
checks complete owned-root evidence without probing saved PIDs or creating
processes. Duplicate roots, changed hashes, reordered history, wrong jobs or
sources, and overlap with closed-body roots fail. Authorization must never have
begun. `grant_issued=False` alone is insufficient: publication can fail after
authorization begins. An authorized or failed body cannot enter this path.

Closed prior searches keep the earlier unstarted-history prefix that their
actual request used. The cost reader checks each endpoint against the roots
known at that endpoint. The next continuation then pays the accumulated known
root costs minus the last controller total; before the first body, it pays all
known unstarted-root costs. Operational histories do not change the method,
data, resource policy, seed, TPE cursor or native recovery identity.

Invocation preflight accepts the explicit resource-only history, authenticates
it after genuine qualification and protects its prior commands/control/intended
worker paths. Older resource requests without the field remain readable as an
empty history; the legacy ungated request cannot acquire this extra field.
Supervisor final receipt publication and caller overhead retain their declared
separate boundary. Missing/unclosed tails remain unavailable, never zero.

The affected five-module suite passed **111 checks in 7.19 seconds**. Actual
cold primary-module fixtures include two pregrant denials and one authorized
body rejected by false qualification before plan/data access. All fit zero
models. Read-only cost reopening forbids process creation; malformed histories,
duplicate costs and the authorized-body exclusion are tested. Initial seven
missing-API setup errors and the first 27-check green run are retained in the
[receipt](WP08-unstarted-primary-cost-receipt.json). No complete qualified public
success was stubbed or run. The mixed complete-qualified search/unstarted-cost
chain still awaits actual qualification; its existing exact body-prefix checks
remain required.
