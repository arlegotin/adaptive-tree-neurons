gates **before** cost inputs or output. They require the exact primary168 and
secondary672 immutable queues, final journals, complete caller/call/invocation
records and owned outer-worker references. Every selected model must belong to
its costed queue search. Every full call and program invocation must have one
committed containing outer interval on an observed matching clock domain.
Missing, orphaned or unclosed evidence prevents complete accounting.

The join keeps full method-call durations separate from inner caller clocks and
common program overhead. It checks each search's paid controller total against
its complete observed calls. Nested intervals are not added twice. Separately
closed execution durations can be summed across boot domains; a combined span
and idle interval are reported only within one observed domain. All durations
use the recorded monotonic clock implementation, rather than UTC timestamps.
The default serial track rejects overlapping outer/call intervals.

The costed practical methods fit their learned parents and native preprocessing
inside their observed calls. Data acquisition, engineering qualification,
observer publication/operator overhead and future protected prediction,
evaluation and deployment remain separately identified study stages. They are
not imputed as zero. This seal authenticates training/search execution only;
whole-pipeline and efficiency claims stay false. It grants no T permission.
The later protected protocol must load this genuine proof and its other gates.

actual queue have no marker and cause no program/plan read. Foreign, changed or
stale markers fail; unknown failure tails remain missing.
