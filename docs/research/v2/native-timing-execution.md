# Original-model timing execution and readback

The public runner and collector first load the actual complete frozen native
plan, then recheck it after execution/readback. A supplied metadata dictionary
or true flag cannot open that route. The complete-qualified public success and
9,360-job benchmark queue remain unrun.

The controller uses the existing immutable workqueue and cooperative process
supervisor. It copies request JSON bytes into each measurement episode and
keeps original absolute model and F-query references. Workers load saved native
configuration/seed and predict; they do not train or read targets. Execution is
serial, with no wall-time cap. The default root RSS pause threshold is4GiB;
its live available-memory proxy is5.5GiB and free-disk proxy512MiB. Admission is
checked before starting a job and before granting its worker. These proxies do
not guarantee allocation. A different declared policy needs its own queue
identity; model/data/effective batch are never silently reduced.

An operational prefix pauses before the next member and resumes the fixed
order. Before-start resource denial consumes no measurement attempt. Completed
members are never remeasured. The single queue recovery may adopt the exact
original process only when its closure, request, samples and call evidence
validate. Ambiguous or partly executed members stay missing. Later members may
finish, but any missing paired member blocks that comparison's paired estimate.
There is no complete-case timing inference or favorable-sample replacement.

The read-only collector authenticates queue inventories, original templates,
process ownership, finite repeated samples, all failed/successful executor
calls and their containing queue/context records. It performs no second model
prediction. Each complete comparison requires30 randomized paired blocks;
batch1 and1,024 retain first-call and mean-of32 resident timings separately
from model load, whole process and whole executor call.

Calls, roots, queue invocations and context invocations nest. They must not be
added together. The final observer publication/return, cold coordinator startup,
native training/search and standalone prerequisites still need a complete
containing observer and cost join. Failed contexts and unknown costs remain
explicit. Native/F-role, dedicated-timing, precision-equivalence and efficiency
flags stay false in the private evidence. A float64 native quality result does
not automatically qualify float32-input deployment quality.

The final seven-module command passed **132 checks in51.25seconds**, including
17 new controller checks. The [receipt](WP10-native-timing-execution-receipt.json)
retains intended RED, the unchanged sandbox-permission recovery, and the
reproduced recovery/context faults and fixes. Counts overlap earlier checks;
this is not a whole-suite pass.

A known-data capture ran60 owned cold processes in one30-pair comparison,
paused after three members, resumed and reopened without new processes. All
original snapshot bytes remained unchanged. Two deliberately failed local
members retained their call costs and produced no paired estimate. The capture
contains two known P13 fixture fits and1,007 inventoried files/logs. Its timing
is contended software evidence, not a benchmark or neuron-method result.

Actual benchmark execution, complete costs, report/CLI/readiness integration
and final review remain before production. No protected benchmark targets,
benchmark inference or new algorithmic rescue was admitted by this component.
