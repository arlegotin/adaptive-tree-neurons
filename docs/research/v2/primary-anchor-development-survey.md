# Primary native-anchor development survey

This decision is written before opening transfer V labels for this survey.
The objective is to inspect existing completed native anchors, using the
primary-only F/G/V permission already specified for WP07. It adds no training,
search-support change, seed selection, refit, protected evaluation or promotion
of an engineering worker to production eligibility.

The finite inventory is the original twenty named native anchors on six
datasets:120cells, all with fit seed17. Families and anchor IDs are those in
`research.search_supports_v2.family_search_spec`: P00/P01 historical selected and
random; P02 lower/upper; P03 published and historical-width/LR under native
preprocessing; P04 ResNet; P05 four static coordinates; P06 RealMLP; P07 plain
and embedded TabM; P08/P09/P10/P11 native tree anchors; P12 full NODE; P13 mean.
P13 here is only the mean anchor, not the full mean/ridge/spline family search.

Use only outer0for nontemporal tasks and Bike outer4. Authenticate the existing
source/queue/closed-worker/prediction commitments and their original primary
F/G/V-feature hashes before scoring. The fit workers read only F/G labels and
V features. The coordinator reads V labels through the existing logged primary
validation reader. It reports group/day-weighted NMSE, F variance ddof0, and
row-weighted RMSE/MAE from `score_primary_validation_prediction`. T features and
labels, and secondary development roles, remain sealed.

Retain all120cells and the complete operational failure/recovery record. A
missing or active anchor stays unavailable, never assigned an artificial risk.
Only a completed native anchor with its original exact config/seed and audited
closed predictions can supply that cell. Same-seed infrastructure reruns already
recorded by their receipts can replace an unavailable attempt; they are not
competing seeds or configurations. Energy is simulation transfer, separate from
the five observational tasks. No statistical superiority or generalization
claim is made from these V outcomes.

The metadata plan and byte commitments must be written before label access. All
currently available cells are scored under that plan without outcome-based
omission. A later completed P12 cell may be scored by a separate immutable
appendix bound to the original120-cell inventory; neither its inclusion nor
training depends on this survey's scores. The original native engineering
source identities and limits remain visible. They are not silently migrated to
the current package or to the final common-source benchmark qualification.

Publish every scored anchor and status. Individual family anchors may be
compared descriptively, but do not call the best V anchor a held-out champion or
the results a completed tuned benchmark. This is development evidence and can
inform only choices already permitted before the global freeze. It does not
unlock an extra HPO restart, invented search range or M3 rescue. The M3 Gate B
retirement remains the completed result of that study. Production WP08 still
requires final native/workload/support qualification, the complete pre-freeze
implementation and the same fixed family/search/seed inventory.
