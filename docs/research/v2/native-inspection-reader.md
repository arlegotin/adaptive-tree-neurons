# Closed primary native search inspections

The generic reader handles both fixed anchors and searched configurations:

```python
load_primary_native_inspection(
    reference, *, generation_arguments, immutable_generation_root)
```

It reopens the same eight-field native generation contract used by the workload
reader. A closed inspection must belong to that exact immutable generation,
source, configuration, fit seed and primary split. The saved independent replay
must use its original snapshot, query and predictions. Actual query/prediction
NPY headers supply dtype and shape checks without array allocation. Managed
commands, environments, owned process records, raw RSS samples and complete
artifact inventories are checked again.

The declared primary coordinator recomputes V arithmetic. Final checks recheck
the generation, source, original inputs, inspection and arithmetic references,
including a label-file change after scoring. The reader fits no model, refits no
transform and makes no new prediction. Protected labels remain unopened. Saved
replay equality is trusted execution of its pinned managed worker; the reader
does not independently repeat inference.

All 54 affected checks passed at the recorded source. Actual fixtures include
a grouped mean anchor, a searched ridge configuration and a temporal primary
fold with day arithmetic. They are synthetic role fixtures, not authentication
of official acquisition. Changed schema, premature authority, score, replay
status, raw resource count, snapshot sidecar and source reject. The dtype
adversarial cases first exposed acceptance of contradictory receipt fields;
after joining actual headers, they reject. Earlier failures remain retained.
The [component receipt](WP08-native-inspection-reader-receipt.json) binds source,
commands, logs and independently reopened fixture observations.

This component grants no software/workload/support qualification, paid journal,
selected-model or protected authority. The unchanged inspected trial bridge
still returns ineligible results with no selectable V score. A separate
production bridge must consume the complete actual
[native registry](native-qualification-registry.md) and each candidate's full
workload before granting companion selection eligibility. Full production
qualification and protocol integration remain unfinished.
