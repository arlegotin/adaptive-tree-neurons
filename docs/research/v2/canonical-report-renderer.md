# Canonical report rendering

`analysis.report_v2.render_report` formats a supplied canonical audit record;
`write_report` publishes it to a fresh file. Both accept completed, estimation,
retired and blocked study states. No confirmation directory is fabricated for
an earlier retired/blocked branch. This component opens no raw data/model files,
authenticates no protocol, runs no inference and performs no literature review.
The future sealed artifact auditor must produce and substantiate its input.

The report preserves the declared final prediction inventory/order, every paid
attempt and failure status, original-unit metrics, normalized risk and independent
unit definitions. Missing predictions and undefined F-variance normalization
remain distinct; neither gets a favorable imputed risk. Supplied comparison
estimates, confidence bounds, margins and unit counts remain visible. A positive
comparison requires nonempty inventories, available planned risks, complete
confirmation scope, all mandatory auditor attestations and a supplied lower
bound strictly above its margin. The renderer does not calculate that interval,
verify its statistical law or replace the auditor's source/model/data checks.

Source, raw-prediction and protected-protocol attestation flags are printed even
when false. Development/software/arithmetic reports explicitly state that they
authorize no protected confirmation. Containing physical and standalone
attributed costs are printed separately and never added. References and
limitations stay visible. Fixed declared ordering, no generated timestamp and
explicit formatting make the output deterministic. Existing outputs are never
overwritten; invalid input produces no report.

Twelve missing-component checks became twelve passing cases. Confidence-interval
fields, undefined normalization, visible unverified provenance and empty-positive-
inventory checks were then exercised and implemented. The final122 affected
report/arithmetic/teacher/practical/cost/design checks passed. All records and
generated reports are sentinels, with no real predictions, confirmation, model
fitting or T access. The canonical artifact auditor, frozen protected reader,
complete study-to-audit conversion, deployment workers and production `report`
CLI remain pending. This is not the completed WP11 independent audit or release.
