`InspectedNativeTrialBridge`. It requires a complete actual

working root, primary fold, manifest, split, family support and current

can grant eligibility for the declared V selection. Original worker, model,
inspection and engineering bridge flags remain unchanged. Failed or interrupted
attempts retain their status, checkpoints and paid cost without a selectable
score.

The registry is reopened deeply once per constructor. Each callback boundary
still checks its immutable manifest, prerequisite byte inventories, current
verification identity and every working role archive. These integrity scans,
candidate checks and native preparation are paid time. Forecast their real
overhead; do not present them as a free method-speed advantage or add nested

component evidence with eligibility flags still false. It is useful for checking
