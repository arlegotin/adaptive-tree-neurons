against the current native-quality plan, source, core/adapter locks and actual
support law. Loading recomputes that exact policy and its input bindings.
Changing and rehashing a deadline, method, family, refit rule, analysis, seed,
barrier or access flag cannot preserve the declaration.

The method is the explicitly unchanged reference: P00 uses the cheap Adam
placement score and P01 uses random placement with the same informed contrasts.
The failed M3 joint selector is not promoted into this practical procedure.
The declaration binds the retained negative M3 and B0 dispositions. It admits
no synthetic calibration, confirmation or protected teacher root.

32 paid attempts, P13's finite 29 configurations and separate 10/30/120-minute
budget views. It fixes F/G/V roles, F-only transforms, retained F-fit/G-selected
models without a silent refit, V ties, both search repetitions, the portfolio's
twelve-search cost and complete failure/missingness treatment.

seed namespace, five observational tasks with Energy separate, group/day weights,
paired resampling, Bike blocks and dependence qualifications. Primary-fold
access and the requirement to freeze all 168 primary models plus the same global
procedure before 672 secondary searches remain explicit.

This is a **pre-search declaration**, not the post-search global freeze or an
access token. All fitting, native qualification, secondary/protected access and
whole-cost authority flags remain false. No role arrays or model processes are
needed to declare metadata. Actual qualification and measured admission still
precede production execution.

this exact declaration after its actual qualification/inventory validation and
rechecks it at the end. Arbitrary global-procedure JSON is insufficient. Complete
actual execution of that 168-model barrier remains unrun. Physical whole-cost
