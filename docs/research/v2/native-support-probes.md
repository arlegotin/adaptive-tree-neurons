# Short exercises of full model geometry

`research.support_probes_v2` adds a fixed engineering catalog for the larger
architectures in the practical search. It does not qualify a model for quality
comparison, admit HPO or change the finite scientific search supports.

`support_probe_cases(family, feature_schema)` retains maximum requested own-model
width128/count fractions, both ordinary capacity brackets, all four static
parameterizations,512-wide MLP/ResNet backbones, all twelve RealMLP
embedding/hidden-layout combinations,1024-wide plain/embedded TabM, the declared
large tree geometries and the16-knot spline design. Both128/256 batch shapes are
retained where the neural search supports them. The catalog covers43 explicit
cases per schema, not every configuration or a proof that memory is monotone in
these coordinates. Native parsers validate every original and translated case.

`plan_support_probes` binds the original primary split, prepared manifest, whole
scientific source, adapter locks, seed17 and exact catalog. `run_support_probe`
stages the complete original F/G and feature-only V archives, then owns one fresh
single-thread worker with an RSS guard and no wall deadline. It checks the actual
F count, closed worker status, finite predictions and exact same-worker snapshot
reload, then rechecks original/staged bytes, source and locks. All consumed time
and failures remain in a fresh output. A failed late check clears saved success
flags. There is no automatic seed replacement or retry.

Optimization is deliberately short and explicitly nonquality: two updates per
own-model stage, one epoch for other neural adapters, two boosting rounds, and
one NODE sampler epoch plus one update at the actual2048-tree/depth6 geometry
and effective512-row batch. P13 performs its ordinary closed fit. Complete F/G
archives and preprocessing are retained; in particular, two own-model updates
do **not** establish full optimization-row coverage. Full native stopping,
training coverage, gradient/shape evidence and recovery remain the separate
anchor/workload requirements. These geometry runs cannot substitute for them.

The own-model engineering schema now admits the family's existing production
widths in addition to its4/8-unit fixtures. LightGBM engineering schemas retain
their old tiny choices and also admit their family's existing production
leaf/minimum-row choices. Production schemas, defaults, stopping rules and
eligibility remain unchanged. Existing native result flags are never flipped.

Only synthetic fixtures exercised the new runner during implementation: a real
P13 ridge fit and a128-unit ordinary warm-growth fit, plus owned RSS failure and
late-byte-corruption checks. The other catalog routes were parsed and checked
for unchanged geometry; they have not all been executed under this component.
No new real-data quality result, V-label access or protected access follows.

The subsequent [actual-input resource tranche](../../../reports/research-v2-resource-probes.md)
completed the full-width ordinary upper bracket on Bike and the largest
embedded TabM geometry on Parkinsons/Bike. The Bike TabM exercise stopped at an
initial2GiB guard, then completed the identical request/seed under4GiB at2.984GiB
sampled root RSS. Both attempts and costs remain. These three short geometries
are useful measurements, not complete catalog/native workload qualification.

The remaining production registry must join final-source software verification,
all native anchors, actual support/workload results and acquisition/data facts.
Neither a declared catalog entry nor a successful short exercise supplies that
authority. Held-source native runs and original public results remain unchanged.

## Reopening a closed exercise

`load_support_probe(reference, prepared_root=..., expected_manifest_sha256=...,
expected_family=..., expected_case_id=...)` accepts an externally committed
`{root, inventory_sha256}` reference. It reconstructs the exact current-source,
lock-bound primary plan and engineering budget, reopens the managed process and
closed-worker records, checks original/staged input and query identities, and
validates finite saved predictions. It launches no fitting or inference and
reads no V labels or T arrays. Its F/G-label and V-feature access is logged.

The reader joins request and snapshot pointers, process start/terminal records,
thread/overlay/argv identity and the full byte inventory. It independently
recomputes the observed RSS peak from the retained samples; a zero sample during
worker exit is allowed when positive observations remain. Sampling is not a
strict peak-memory guarantee or aggregate descendant-memory measurement.
Late source/role/proof changes prevent a checked return. A resource-stopped or
otherwise unsuccessful exercise cannot supply a passed geometry proof.

The returned record explicitly leaves full native training, complete row-visit
authentication, workload/quality eligibility, HPO and protected authority false.
Original managed training and same-worker snapshot reload are trusted records,
not newly executed or independently replayed by this reader. It supplies one
checked input to the future qualification registry. Old held-source geometry
measurements cannot silently become current-source proofs after a core change.
