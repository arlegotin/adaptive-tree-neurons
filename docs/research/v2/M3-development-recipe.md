# M3 development recipe decision

This is a prospective builder decision, made before generating or fitting any
M3 teacher. It locks the common treatment for the finite development study; it
is not a protected confirmation or global source freeze. The complete M2 report
is the evidence for the decision. No M3 outcome informed it.

The objective is whether joint allocation beats informed-direction random after
competent ordinary training. M2e's best single global V recipe among the five
declared ordinary recipes used weight decay1e-3. Independently, M2c's four-teacher
fixed-horizon diagnostic found about16.2% lower mean exploratory E risk for
unchanged continuation at8000updates versus its stage-zero parent. These are
different populations/treatments; their means cannot be compared or their
benefits added. Their interaction is unmeasured. Earlier gated growth was often
worse, so M3 uses the late selected parent.

The builder therefore chooses this common M3 treatment:

- Preserve primary d16/h64, requested count16, sigma.1, F2306/G577/V577,
  exploratory E65536, current F-derived contrasts epsilon.05/tau1, batch128,
  AdamW learning rate.005, constant absolute schedule and inherited moments.
- Use weight decay1e-3 for every parent and fitted arm. Fit each parent under
  the8000-update ceiling, G every25/patience20 and complete strict-G-best restore.
- From that late parent, execute a fixed8000-update continuation for every warm
  arm, without patience termination. Include G at update0 and every25updates;
  retain the complete strict-G-best state across the common horizon. Keep uses
  that same horizon. Preserve the selected parent's sampler stream and absolute
  schedule; no stream, optimizer or schedule restart.
- Fresh ordinary upper-bracket fits use one fixed8000-update stage, the same
  regularization/optimizer/schedule/G grid, and independently declared fresh
  initialization. They have a different inheritance/total-training cost, which
  must remain explicit. Warm ordinary upper brackets use the shared parent and
  common continuation. Resolve upper width from actual effective gate count;
  the nominal primary width is95.
- Singleton structural probes use fixed300updates, G0/25/.../300, existing
  contrasts and the same parent/regularization. Include keep; select IDs by G
  benefit and stable ties. Discard their learned weights before the final joint
  continuation. Every standalone search procedure pays all probes.

This changes the handoff's initial legacy-treatment M3 coordinate for a named
reason: native patience replay concealed measured ordinary headroom. It avoids
interpreting repair of that treatment as useful capacity. It does not claim that
the combined ordinary recipe is optimal, or that fixed8000 is a deployable native
recipe for every practical baseline. It is a finite controlled mechanism
horizon, not an arbitrary wall-time cutoff. Native practical methods retain
their own training procedures under the results-first resource amendment.
