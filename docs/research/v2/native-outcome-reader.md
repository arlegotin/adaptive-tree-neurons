# Closed native fit outcomes

`inspect_native_fit_outcome` joins the original F/G/V-features request, closed
native result, managed process invocation, raw RSS observations and worker
terminal when present. It preserves native stopping, source, configuration and
seed. It launches no process, fits no model and reads no V/T labels.

Normal completion remains an engineering model. Resource exhaustion, interruption
and numerical/dependency failures retain their operational status. Failure never
becomes an arbitrary prediction loss. The reader requires complete closed
records: a disk-full attempt missing its terminal receipt stays incomplete.
It cannot reconstruct an unmeasured cost tail as zero.

The [paid-search auditor component](qualified-search-audit.md) now consumes this
evidence. The outcome reader itself grants no native
workload, model eligibility, independent training replay, search recovery lineage,
whole-search timing or protected access. The exact journal must still bind any
restart/recovery to the preceding paid attempt, and successful candidates need
their full workload, replay and V checks plus actual registry qualification.

The scoped suite passed 43 checks in 21.87 seconds. Actual tiny native mean and
owned RSS-stop records were reopened read-only. Three additional faults exposed
missing clock-scope/type/status validation; their failures and fixes are retained
alongside the initial missing-interface failures. The
[receipt](WP08-native-outcome-reader-receipt.json) binds source, commands, logs and
the actual observations. These checks are not a whole-suite or final-source
qualification, and produce no new scientific result.
