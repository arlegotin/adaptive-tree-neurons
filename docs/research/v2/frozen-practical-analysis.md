# Frozen practical comparison binding

The public seal and loader require the actual final protocol, complete
prediction-stage proof and witnessed metric completion before reading scoring
evidence or creating output. They revalidate after analysis. Neither route
opens label arrays or loads models. Complete-qualified public success remains
unrun; numeric fixtures grant no native or protected-data authority.

The compiler preserves all30 folds,840 procedure jobs and3,360 budget views.
Every available view binds an exact query, split, family, repeat and saved
model. Every unavailable view retains its status. It checks group/day losses
against each fixed normalized risk; zero F variance stays undefined. An
incomplete family inventory cannot shrink into a supposedly complete comparison.

Only fold0, or Bike fold4, drives primary comparisons. Other folds remain
descriptive. The baseline champion comes from the frozen V-only P02–P13 choice
for each dataset/repeat/view, including its exact job/model binding. A missing
portfolio member prevents a complete champion. Test outcomes never select it.

The existing conditional comparison implementation averages the two risks
before taking their log ratio, weights the five observational tasks equally,
and keeps Energy separate. It uses the frozen20,000 paired resamples and seed,
the seven-day Bike block plus3/14-day sensitivities, leave-one-group checks and
the13-family Holm procedure. All contrasts reuse the same resampled units.
The10/30/120-minute prefixes report points descriptively. Undefined Energy log
ratios do not suppress valid observational point summaries.

The output retains group-loss inputs, policy seed/law, iterations and pairing
hashes. Bootstrap draw vectors regenerate from those inputs and the frozen
source; they are omitted from compact JSON. No resampling law or observation is
changed to obtain a favorable result.

The hand-checked example has proposed repeat risks[1,3] and a V-selected
champion with risks[2,6]. Its benefit is log2; the fixed family with risks[2,8]
has benefit log2.5. This is an arithmetic example, not a benchmark improvement.

The final seven-module command passed **151 checks in87.74seconds**, including
the18 new binding checks. Initial RED failures and corrected portfolio/Energy
boundary cases are retained in the [receipt](WP10-frozen-practical-analysis-receipt.json).
The capture contains numeric metadata only: no fitted/loaded model, label
archive access or complete-qualified public positive. Counts overlap earlier
verification and are not a whole-suite pass.

Independent raw target/model/access audit, dependence qualifications, complete
costs/deployment, final report/CLI/readiness and branch review remain required
before production. Conditional bootstrap arithmetic does not establish
superiority over a population of datasets.
