# Known caller intervals in search costs

The owned caller now publishes a separate immutable `caller-complete.json`
after its episode outcome. Its observed endpoint includes publishing and hashing
the episode, plus preparing the completion record. Its own final publication and
return remain outside that endpoint. A future physical study clock must cover
that bookkeeping; this component grants no complete-cost or efficiency claim.

Resource-bound prior histories may attach this exact completion reference to a
closed body or never-authorized root. The read-only boundary reader reopens the
owned cooperative evidence, request, caller header and episode outcome. It checks
their bytes, source, command, environment, identities, dispositions and nested
clock relationships. It neither starts processes nor probes saved PIDs.

The continuation uses the validated caller interval instead of adding it to the
nested root interval. It pays the accumulated known unique episodes minus the
last controller total before new candidate-prefix eligibility. Old root-only
records remain readable, with explicit root-only counts. Missing caller work is
not assigned zero. Prior caller directories are also protected against output
reuse. Native quality and whole-procedure authority remain separate.

The final seven-module verification passed **136 checks in 8.27 seconds**.
Earlier failures exposed missing interfaces, an endpoint that omitted reference
hashing, and insufficient protection/validation of linked caller records. The
[receipt](WP08-caller-cost-boundary-receipt.json) retains those failures and the
final command/source identity. A read-only capture of a real primary-command
fixture, denied before process creation, joins its observed 42,499,959 ns caller
interval, including 10,883,042 ns beyond the nested root. These are software
fixture measurements, not native training or benchmark timing results.

No complete-qualified public success or mixed qualified-body/unstarted chain
was fabricated or run. Production queue/readiness, the physical whole-cost
audit, global procedure freeze, secondary release and protected evaluation
remain unfinished. Existing predictive findings and held-source fits are
unchanged.
