# Practical pipeline cost accounting

The fixed programme now binds a sibling observer archive. Public confirmation
workers use its locked, contiguous episode slots. The cost reader discovers
every slot, including denied and failed workers; callers cannot supply a
success-only list. Missing terminals, unknown artifacts and orphaned history
block complete costs. Legacy pre-test search and private software fixtures
retain their existing paths and guards.

The public cost seal/loader first reopens the actual frozen native/model,
protocol, prediction, metrics, analysis, raw-audit, timing-plan and pre-test
cost evidence. It checks the protected owner's exact programme reference and
all seven completed operations. It then routes each invocation and full step
call to exactly one committed containing worker on the same observed clock
domain. Overlapping serial frames/invocations, missing operations and unknown
clock domains reject. Closed elapsed durations may sum across boots; absolute
calendar spans do not.

Prediction costs include every successful and failed call, mapped to its
frozen query and procedure job. Aliased native/prefix views reuse one query.
A missing planned query is never assigned zero. A job with no planned query
has that status explicitly. The result separates840 standalone training/search
procedures plus all their frozen query costs, common evaluation/timing-study
work, and actual physical programme frames. These are different views and
must not be added together. The12-family validation portfolio sums all its
family searches; its cost does not belong to one baseline family.

Nested query, scoring, audit and native-measurement clocks must fall within
the corresponding fixed-operation calls. Training parents, preprocessing,
probes and failed attempts remain in the original full training calls. Common
engineering/data acquisition/qualification work and observer/final cost/report
administration remain separately reported or unknown, never zero-imputed.
Dedicated timing and float32 deployment-quality equivalence remain unqualified,
so the implementation enables no formal efficiency claim on its own.
